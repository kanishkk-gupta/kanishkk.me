# EYECONIC

> "What if glasses could do more than just help people see?"

That's the question that started everything.

---

## What is EyeConic?

EyeConic is an AI-powered smart safety eyewear platform that combines Artificial Intelligence, IoT and wearable technology into everyday glasses.

The goal wasn't to create another fancy gadget.

The goal was to build something people would actually wear every day that could genuinely improve their safety.

Today it's a patented prototype.

Tomorrow...

Hopefully something millions of people wear.

---

## Who built EyeConic?

EyeConic was built by:

- Kanishk Gupta
- Anurag Kumar
- Shreshth Mittal
- Raghav Khandelwal

It started as a college innovation project...

Eventually became much bigger.

---

## How did the idea start?

Initially...

We simply noticed something.

Millions of people wear glasses.

Yet...

They're still just glasses.

We started asking ourselves...

> "What if glasses could actually help people?"

Around the same time...

Women's safety was becoming an increasingly concerning issue.

That pushed us toward building something capable of helping people during emergencies.

Then life happened.

One day my phone got stolen at MG Metro Station.

That completely changed how we looked at personal safety.

Instead of solving one problem...

Why not solve multiple?

Then another challenge appeared.

What if someone attacks the user...

The glasses fall...

How would they send their location?

That question eventually led us to Anti-Fall Detection.

Later we realized...

That same feature could help elderly people as well.

One question kept leading to another.

That's how EyeConic evolved.

---

## What problem does EyeConic solve?

EyeConic isn't trying to solve just one problem.

It's trying to become an intelligent wearable safety companion.

Some of its goals include:

- Personal safety
- Emergency SOS
- Anti-theft protection
- Voice transcription
- Wearable AI assistance
- Fall detection
- Future accessibility features

Instead of carrying multiple gadgets...

Everything becomes part of something millions already wear.

---

## Mobile Theft Protection

One feature was inspired by my own experience.

If your phone gets separated from you...

EyeConic immediately detects it.

The glasses vibrate.

Even if your phone is on silent mode...

The phone overrides silent mode and starts ringing loudly until authenticated.

Simple idea.

Real problem.

---

## Women's Safety

EyeConic was designed with emergency situations in mind.

The goal is to make it easier for someone to quickly request help when they don't have time to unlock a phone.

Safety shouldn't require opening an app.

## Anti-Fall Detection
